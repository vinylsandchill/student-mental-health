University life can be challenging, and looking after your mental health isn't always straightforward. This student-focused resource offers practical, judgement-free guidance to help you recognise difficulties and support your wellbeing.

Whether you're checking in with yourself, finding things difficult, or needing urgent support, you can explore this resource at your own pace. Your needs may change from day to day and you don't have to wait until things become urgent to ask for help.

Not sure where to begin?

All three pathways are open to everyone. You can also browse support services without choosing a pathway.
